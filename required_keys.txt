  What it buys you: the viva (phase 2). One call per page of material to write
  its questions — once, for every student who vivas on it — and one call per
  answer to mark it. The examiner's other words are written without a model.

  Without it: vivas still run. Questions are written by rule from what a page
  states outright (definitions; a lab manual's Aim, Principle, Result), and
  answers are marked by matching their words to the page. The report says so.

  One key. Groq's free tier is 8K tokens a minute and 200K a day per model;
  do not pool keys from different organisations (Groq's terms forbid it).

  Optional: M20_MODEL_BANK / M20_MODEL_MARKING move one job to another model
  (any id `ai_core.models` knows), without moving the rest of the platform.
